# Module attribution

Each curated module in this directory is adapted from a community blueprint, credited below to its original
author (SPEC §5.4, D19: attribution is mandatory). The originals are on factorioprints.com, which states no
licence: the authors keep their rights, and this project's MIT licence does not cover these modules. The
original blueprint strings are cached unchanged in `sources/`. The adaptations bring each blueprint to
early-game tech for Rival Engineers (machine and inserter tiers, recipes) and add the ports the planner
connects to; each module's `notes` list every change from the original.
